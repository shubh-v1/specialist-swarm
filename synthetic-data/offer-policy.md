## 4. Approval matrix (summary)

| Condition | Required approvers |

An offer that went out without the required approvals must be **ratified retroactively before the start date**. Payroll will not process the first salary or sign-on bonus until approvals are complete.

## 5. Reference & background checks

1. **Minimum references:** 2 completed references, of which **at least one must be a current or former direct manager**.
2. **Senior roles (L5+):** 3 references requested; 2 completed (incl. one manager) is the minimum to start.
3. **Deadline:** references must be completed **no later than 5 business days before the start date**.
4. **Current-employer references:** may only be contacted after the candidate has resigned. If not reachable within 10 business days of resignation, the recruiter may close it as "unable to reach" provided rule 1 is still met.
5. **Background check (Germany):** criminal record certificate ("Führungszeugnis") required. If not received by start date, HR may approve a **conditional start**: employee starts, but IT provisions only baseline accounts (no production, no customer data) until received.
6. **Right to work:** must be verified before start. No exceptions.

## 6. Recruiter hand-off checklist (to Onboarding)

- [ ] Signed offer letter on file
- [ ] All approvals per §4 recorded in Workday
- [ ] Reference requirements per §5 met (or exception documented)
- [ ] Background check received, or conditional start approved
- [ ] Right-to-work verified
- [ ] Start date and notice period confirmed
