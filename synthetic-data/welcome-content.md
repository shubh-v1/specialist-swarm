## 1. Who we are

BTS-Synthetic builds an enterprise data platform for industrial and IoT companies: real-time ingest, a governed lakehouse, and analytics on top. ~1,100 people across Austin (HQ), Munich, Berlin, Bucharest, and London. Founded 2017.

**Mission:** Make every sensor reading useful within a second of it happening.

## 2. Our values

| Value | What it looks like day to day |
| --- | --- |
| **Ship small, ship often** | We'd rather merge a small PR today than a perfect one next month. New hires ship something in week 1. |
| **Write it down** | Decisions live in docs (RFCs, ADRs), not in meetings. Async first, meetings for discussion. |
| **Own the outcome** | You build it, you run it. Every engineer joins on-call after onboarding. |
| **Be kind, be direct** | Candid feedback, delivered with care. |
| **Customers' data, customers' trust** | Security and privacy are part of the definition of done. |

## 3. Ways of working

- **Hybrid:** office days are team-agreed (Data Platform Munich: Tue/Wed/Thu). Core collaboration hours 10:00–16:00 local.
- **Tools:** Slack for chat, Confluence for docs, Jira for work, GitHub for code, Google-free (we're a Microsoft 365 shop).
- **Meetings:** no-meeting Fridays. Every meeting needs an agenda doc.
- **Engineering onboarding path:** Week 1 — environment + first PR. Weeks 2–4 — pod onboarding project. Week 4 — prod read access. Week 6–8 — shadow on-call, then join rotation.

## 8. Tone guide for welcome content

Warm, direct, specific. Use the new hire's preferred name. Mention one or two personal interests from their profile when there's a genuine connection (e.g. a Slack channel or club). Avoid corporate filler ("we're thrilled to have you onboard on this exciting journey"). Keep the welcome letter under 250 words.
